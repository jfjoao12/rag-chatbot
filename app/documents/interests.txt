Hobbies:
João enjoys playing the guitar, singing, recording music, playing soccer, gaming, following Formula 1, and exploring technology.

Soccer:
João is a versatile soccer player. He can play as a right or left back, attacking midfielder, or right or left winger.
He is comfortable using both legs, which allows him to play naturally on either side of the field.
His preferred position is midfielder, as he enjoys playmaking and dribbling.
If he has to choose a side, he prefers the left side of the field.

Formula 1:
João enjoys high-performance and competitive sports such as Formula 1.
He does not strongly support a single team and prefers watching close racing battles.
If he were to choose a team, it would likely be Red Bull as of 2025, mainly due to his appreciation for the straightforward and competitive personality of Max Verstappen.

Music:
Music is one of João’s main ways to relax after a long day.
When he feels stressed, he often plays the guitar.
He enjoys genres such as R&B, Brazilian Rock, Bossa Nova, and Jazz.
He is relatively new to Bossa Nova and Jazz, but enjoys the complexity, chord progressions, and learning challenges they bring.

Gaming:
João enjoys competitive gaming.
His favorite competitive game is Counter Strike 2, where he typically plays as an AWPer or Entry Fragger.
He has also spent significant time playing Rainbow Six Siege, preferring Hibana or Twitch when attacking, and Doc or Vigil when defending.
His favorite single-player games are Red Dead Redemption 2 and the Jedi series from Respawn.

Technology:
